# Transactions Overview

## Definition

Transactions represent financial movements in Poolaki and are divided into four main types:

- **Incomes:** Money received into the account.

- **Expenses:** Money spent out of the account.

- **Savings**: also called contributions. Money moved from the general account into a specific savings goal.

- **Withdrawals:** Money moved out of a savings goal and back into the general
  account. Withdrawals are available for active goals and cannot exceed the
  goal's current balance.

## Permissions and Collaboration

- All members of the account can view all transactions.

- Only the member who originally created a transaction is able to edit or delete it.

- Once a new transaction is created, all other members are notified.

## Transaction Details Visible

Each transaction entry displays the following information:

- Date
- Name / Description
- Category
- Value / Amount

## Creation, Editing, and Default Behavior

- Transactions can be created and edited from the home page or the transactions page.

- Categories are optional when creating or editing transactions.

- Expenses are deducted from the general balance by default, unless explicitly associated with a specific savings goal.

- Expense transactions include an option to mark them as tax refundable.

- Contributions and withdrawals must be linked to an active savings goal.

- An expense can be linked to a completed goal or to an active permanent goal
  (a goal without a target date). Expenses cannot be linked to archived goals
  or to active goals with a target date.

- The general balance and each goal balance are calculated from the effective
  transaction records. They are not independent editable counters.

## Where Transactions Appear and How to Filter Them

- **Transactions Page:** Lists all transactions or specific types. Users can sort items from newest to oldest and vice versa.

- **Filtering Options:** Transactions can be filtered by specific date ranges, by transaction type, by categories (for incomes and expenses), and by the tax refundable status.

- **Reports Page:** Displays an annual filter where a chart represents transactions month by month, showing monthly totals. Detailed records can then be examined on the transactions page.

## Shared account behavior

- Every member of a shared account can view its transactions.

- Only the member who created a transaction can edit or delete it.

- Creating a transaction notifies every other member of the shared account; the
  creator is not notified. Personal budgets do not generate this notification.

- Deleting or editing a goal-linked transaction must preserve the goal balance
  rules. Transactions associated with archived goals cannot be deleted.
